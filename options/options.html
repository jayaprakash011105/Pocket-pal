Pocket Pal

Settings & customization

General

Behaviour

0 disables auto-sleep.

Higher sensitivity needs a shorter press to drag or open the menu.

Extra pals are the next unlocked ones in the roster.

Tip: use the right-click menu or the popup for Play catch. Drag and throw the ball and your pals will chase it.

Tip: press Alt+Shift+P (change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts) to hide or show your pal on every tab.

Characters

Outfits & style

Outfits

One tap to dress your pal up. You can still mix and match below.

Accessories

Pick pieces for the active pal — one head item, one face item and one neck item at a time.

Christmas theme

A living winter wonderland on every page: falling snow that piles up along the bottom, a sleigh, a snowman game and a little snowplow. Switch the theme on here or from the Themes tab of the toolbar popup.

Fine tuning

Custom models

Upload your own glTF/GLB character. It keeps whatever animation clips it ships with (named like "walk", "idle", "jump", …) and Pocket Pal plays the best match for each behaviour; without matching clips it will still move, sit and jump, just without per-limb animation.
